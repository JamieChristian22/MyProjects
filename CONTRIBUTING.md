Contributions & Usage

Thank you for reviewing this portfolio. The repository contains a mix of original case studies and template artifacts. By default:
- Templates and documentation are licensed under the top-level LICENSE (MIT).
- Analytics workbooks (.pbix/.twbx) are included as previews (PNGs/PDFs) in the repo. Full binary workbooks may be available in Releases.

How to contribute
1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a branch: `fix/<short-description>`.
3. Open a pull request describing the change and referencing relevant folders.
4. For large assets, attach them to a Release or provide a link to cloud storage rather than committing binaries to main.
